# Qüstions / Filter criteria

1. What is your primary goal? (learning intention)?
2. What kind of knowledge do you primarily teach (nature of the content)?
3. What are students supposed to do with your media (how active students should be)?
4. How much time do your students have for the media?
5. How big is the group you are teaching (class size)?
6. How do you employ the media: in class, as preparation, self study...
